maptotrack
Offline demo mode: synthetic roads, buildings and elevation.

1 Location

    Route
    How to draw it Click the map to add waypoints at the end. To add a point in the middle, click on the track (or drag a small + handle, or use + in the list). Drag a point to move it, right-click it to delete it. Each segment follows the roads or is a free line: click its pill in the list to switch.
      Area
      How it works Every drivable street inside the area becomes road, with width and markings read on the photo, smooth heights and junctions with rounded kerbs. Rectangle: drag it on the map. Polygon: click its corners, then click the first one (or double-click) to finish; drag a corner to move it, right-click it to remove it, drag a small + to add one. At most 10 km² and 6 km across; above 4 km² the ground texture gets coarser so the track stays light. Start spot: click it, then click the street where the cars should start (otherwise the main street near the middle).
